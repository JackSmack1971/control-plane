# Propose evidence-based improvement

Do not use for one-off preference changes, self-approval, or unsupported policy expansion.

Inputs: observed problem, evidence sources, and candidate artifact area.

1. Collect the independent evidence required by `manifest.yaml`.
2. Classify the remedy as rule, skill, workflow, hook, script, or no change.
3. State write set, risks, and deterministic validation before proposing a transaction.

Output: evidence-backed proposal or explicit no-change decision. On insufficient evidence, defer it. Validate evidence count and the selected artifact boundary.
